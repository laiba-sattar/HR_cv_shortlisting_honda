Mechanical Design Engineer
Honda Atlas Cars (Pakistan) Limited — Manufacturing Division, Lahore

About the role
We are seeking a Mechanical Design Engineer to join our product development
team. The successful candidate will work on component design for passenger
vehicle assemblies, supporting the localisation of parts and continuous
improvement of existing designs.

Responsibilities
- Produce 3D models and 2D manufacturing drawings for vehicle components.
- Apply GD&T standards to drawings and review supplier submissions.
- Support design validation, tolerance stack-up analysis and DFM reviews.
- Work with the production and quality teams to resolve manufacturing issues.
- Contribute to cost reduction and part localisation projects.

Required qualification
Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from an HEC-recognised university.

Experience
2-4 years of relevant experience in automotive, manufacturing or product
design. Candidates with strong internship experience in the automotive sector
will also be considered.

Age
Applicants should be between 22 and 30 years of age.

Required skills
- AutoCAD
- SolidWorks
- GD&T
- Design for Manufacturing (DFM)
- Technical Documentation

Preferred skills
- CATIA
- Six Sigma
- Tolerance stack-up analysis
