About the job
Honda Atlas Cars (Pakistan) Ltd. is looking for a driven and capable individual to join our growing team of automotive professionals.

Position Title: Assistant Manager – Accounts Payable
Qualification: ACCA / ACMA or equivalent degree in Accounting / Finance
Experience: 4-5 years
Location: Multan Road - Lahore

Position Summery:
The Assistant Manager – Accounts Payable performs/manages day-to-day accounts payable operations, including invoice processing, expense verification, and payment documentation. The role ensures accuracy, timeliness, and compliance in handling payables related to raw materials, tooling, services, and indirect expenses critical to automotive manufacturing.

Key Responsibilities:

1.Invoice Processing & Verification
•Receive and verify vendor invoices for materials, services, and consumables.
•Perform 2-way/3-way matching with Purchase Orders (PO), Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), and service entries.
•Check tax compliance.
•Support accurate invoice posting in ERP (SAP).

2.Payment Preparation & Support
•Assist in preparing payment runs.
•Compile supporting documents for approvals (PO copies, GRN, comparison sheets).

3.Record Keeping & Documentation
•Maintain organized digital and physical records of invoices, debit notes, and credit notes.
•Support month-end closing activities, including accruals and AP schedules.
•Ensure compliance with internal controls and company policies.

4.Audit & Compliance Support
•Assist in providing documents and explanations for internal, statutory, and external audits.
•Ensure adherence to GST and internal financial control requirements.

Skills & Competencies:
Technical Skills
•Understanding of accounting principles and accounts payable processes.
•Familiarity with GST concepts.
•Proficiency in ERP systems (SAP) and MS Excel, including basic formulas and data entry.
•Strong documentation and numerical accuracy.

Behavioral Competencies
•Good communication and follow-up skills.
•Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
•Strong team player with a proactive and disciplined approach.
•High level of integrity and attention to detail.
